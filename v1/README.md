# v1 prototype (built)

The first ANCHOR was a low cost proof of concept (about ₹2,200) built for a college design thinking course.

- ESP32-S3 DevKitC-1 N16R8, 2 x INMP441 mics (REF outside, ERR inside), MAX98357A amp, 40 mm 8 ohm speaker, red industrial earmuff
- Mono: one cup does the cancelling
- Noise cancelling works on steady machine hum (not measured in dB)
- Mic to speaker delay measured at 3.5 to 4.5 ms
- Breadboard and jumper wires on the headband, powered from a USB power bank
