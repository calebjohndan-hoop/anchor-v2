Wiring (one I2S port shared by everything):
  GPIO 5  -> BCLK of MAX98357A + SCK of both mics
  GPIO 6  -> LRCLK of MAX98357A + WS of both mics
  GPIO 7  -> DIN of MAX98357A
  GPIO 15 <- SD of both mics (joined)
  3V3 -> VDD of both mics                5V -> MAX98357A VIN
  GND -> all GND pins
  REF (outside) mic L/R -> GND           ERR (inside) mic L/R -> 3.3 V
  MAX98357A: SD -> its VIN, GAIN -> its VIN (6 dB, quietest)
  Speaker: the two wires into the MAX98357A screw terminal ONLY (never to GND)
  All sketches start at OUT_VOLUME 20. The MAX98357A is loud - raise slowly.
